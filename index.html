🏨 Stay: The Hosteller Rishikesh

Base is a calm backpacker-style hostel in Upper Tapovan, away from main-road chaos but close enough to walk to the bridges and cafés.

Social common areas, rooftop corners, and an in-house café make it an easy plug-and-play base after wedding exhaustion.

🔗 Official site: The Hosteller

✅ Why this works for the group

  • Backpacker vibe but clean, cozy, and structured
  • Rooftop + greenery = gentle morning and late-night hangouts
  • ~15 minutes walk to Lakshman Jhula; Ram Jhula slightly further
  • WiFi, hot water, AC dorms; paid parking close by

⚠️ Soft Logistics

📍 Things to remember

Uphill stretch: Last ~400–450m is an uphill walk from the main road; comfortable shoes help.

Food policy: Outside food typically discouraged inside; cafés nearby and in-house kitchen cover most cravings.

Bookings: February fills early; locking beds in advance keeps things stress-free.

🗺️ Core Experiences

🕯️ Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat

Time: Roughly 5:30–6:30 PM in February (arrive early for a calm spot).

Why: Soft chants, lit diyas, floating lamps, and golden reflections on the river—probably the emotional core of the trip.

Cost: Free; optional small donation.

🔗 Triveni Ghat on Google Maps

🌉 Lakshman Jhula & Ram Jhula

Why: Iconic suspension bridges with gentle river wind and views of the hills; great for slow walks and photos.

Time needed: 1–2 hours including pauses, shops, and photos.

🔗 Lakshman Jhula – Maps  |  Ram Jhula – Maps

🎨 Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia)

Why: Old meditation site filled with graffiti, quotes, and Beatles-era energy; great for relaxed wandering and photos.

Entry: Around ₹150 per person; allow 1–1.5 hours inside.

✅ Pre-Trip Calm Checklist

📋 Book before you go

  • Dorm beds at The Hosteller for Feb 15–17.
  • Car (Ertiga / equivalent) blocked for all 3 days.
  • Rough parking plan near Upper Tapovan.
  • Share this page / PDF with the full group.

🎒 Pack light but smart

  • Comfortable walking shoes (for uphill + bridges).
  • Light layer / hoodie for mornings and evenings.
  • Sunscreen, cap, sunglasses, lip balm.
  • Swimwear / quick-dry clothes for riverside sitting.
  • Phone charger + power bank.
  • ₹2,000–3,000 cash for autos, tips, and small shops.
  • Reusable bottle + small day backpack.

🚗 Before leaving Delhi

  • Fuel up, check tyre pressure, and basic fluids.
  • Keep offline Maps saved for Rishikesh area.
  • Share live location / route with one trusted person.
  • Download calm playlists and podcasts beforehand.
